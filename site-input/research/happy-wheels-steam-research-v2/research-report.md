# Happy Wheels Steam Research Pack v2

## What changed from v1

v2 completes the new Hotword pre-build workflow:

1. Reuses the existing candidate research instead of doing another broad research cycle.
2. Performs exactly one final 3-query SERP closeout:
   - `Happy Wheels Steam`
   - `Happy Wheels Steam Workshop`
   - `Happy Wheels Steam old account levels`
3. Shrinks launch scope from every writable page to Homepage + the strongest 5 Steam-specific intent pages.
4. Moves system requirements and generic Level Editor/Browser coverage to verified backlog.
5. Keeps price, achievements, exact controls and roster in `RESEARCH_REQUIRED`.
6. Marks the research phase closed for broad research. From here, only page-specific or launch-day delta research is allowed.

## 3-query conclusion

### 1. Happy Wheels Steam

The brand SERP is already filling with:
- Steam metadata,
- Reddit announcement threads,
- fresh news/overview articles.

Competition is no longer “empty,” but there is still no dominant specialist guide domain for the 2026 Steam edition.

### 2. Happy Wheels Steam Workshop

This is the strongest problem gap.

Players explicitly want Workshop support. Search results are dominated by community speculation and low-quality/unsupported claims. The official Steam page confirms:
- Level Editor,
- Level Browser,
- 5M+ user-created levels,

but does **not** confirm Steam Workshop.

That makes a dated “what is actually confirmed” page useful immediately.

### 3. Happy Wheels Steam old account levels

Players care whether their old community history survives the Steam move.

The official Steam page confirms a 5M+ level archive, but does not confirm:
- old TotalJerkface login linking,
- favorites,
- history,
- ownership of old uploads.

SERP lacks a clean authoritative distinction between “the archive is there” and “your personal account data carries over.”

## Final launch scope

Launch with:

- Homepage
- `/steam-release/`
- `/steam-workshop/`
- `/steam-account-levels/`
- `/steam-controller-support/`
- `/steam-deck/`

Do not launch broad Wiki categories.

Do not create system-requirements or generic Level Editor pages merely because they are easy to write. Fold those verified facts into the launch pages until GSC proves separate intent.

## Research phase closure

No more broad Happy Wheels research before build.

Next research triggers are only:
- a launch-page-specific evidence gap,
- official new announcement,
- Sep 21 release-state changes,
- GSC query/impression signals after publishing.
